# 🛞 Monster Truck Crafting Guide & Blueprint

Welcome, builders! Here is your official recipe sheet to build the **Monster Truck** in Survival Mode.

---

## 🧰 Ingredients Shopping List

To build one Monster Truck, you will need to gather these items from your mines and workshop:

| Item | Quantity | How to Get It | Role in the Truck |
|---|:---:|---|---|
| 🧱 **Iron Block** | **2** | 18 Iron Ingots (smelted from raw iron) | Heavy chassis & armored frame |
| 🪟 **Glass Block** | **1** | Smelt 1 Sand block in a furnace | Cabin windshield |
| 🪙 **Iron Ingot** | **1** | Smelt 1 Raw Iron | Front bumper & trim |
| 🌋 **Blast Furnace** | **1** | 5 Iron Ingots + 1 Furnace + 3 Smooth Stone | The big V8 engine block |
| 🏇 **Saddle** | **1** | Found in Dungeons, Temples, or Fishing | Comfy driver & passenger seats |
| ⬛ **Block of Coal** | **2** | 18 Coal chunks from the mines | Giant rubber monster truck tires |
| 🔴 **Redstone Block** | **1** | 9 Redstone Dust from deep mining | High-voltage battery & transmission |

---

## 🔨 Crafting Table 3x3 Grid Layout

Open a standard **Crafting Table** and place the items in this exact layout:

```
+------------------+------------------+------------------+
|   IRON BLOCK     |      GLASS       |   IRON BLOCK     |
|   (Left Armor)   |   (Windshield)   |  (Right Armor)   |
+------------------+------------------+------------------+
|   IRON INGOT     |  BLAST FURNACE   |      SADDLE      |
|     (Bumper)     |  (Heavy Engine)  |     (Seats)      |
+------------------+------------------+------------------+
|  BLOCK OF COAL   |  REDSTONE BLOCK  |  BLOCK OF COAL   |
|   (Left Tires)   | (Battery Power)  |  (Right Tires)   |
+------------------+------------------+------------------+
```

## 🏁 Vehicle Stats Card

- **Max Speed**: 0.40 (Faster than horses and camels!)
- **Auto-Step**: 1 Full Block (Drives straight up blocks without needing ramps or jumping!)
- **Health**: 250 HP (Tough as an Iron Golem!)
- **Seats**: 2 Players (1 Driver + 1 Passenger for buddy road trips!)
- **Control**: Standard WASD keys or joystick to steer.
