# Survival Durability and Drop Mechanics

We decided to set vehicle health to 250 HP and implement dual-mode drop behavior. The heavy health pool ensures the monster truck withstands rough terrain, falls, and mob collisions. When deliberately dismantled by a player, it drops the complete vehicle item for easy redeployment; when destroyed by catastrophic damage (explosions, lava, or combat death), it degrades into salvaged scrap metal.
