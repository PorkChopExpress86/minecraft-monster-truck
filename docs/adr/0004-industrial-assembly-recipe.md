# Industrial Assembly Recipe for Survival Mode

We decided to require an endgame, heavy industrial crafting recipe for the Monster Truck in Survival mode (`recipes/monster_truck.json`): 2 Iron Blocks, 1 Glass Block, 1 Iron Ingot, 1 Blast Furnace (representing the heavy engine), 1 Saddle, 2 Blocks of Coal (representing the massive rubber tires), and 1 Redstone Block. This ensures the 250 HP, obstacle-climbing vehicle serves as a high-value survival achievement rather than an easily spammed early-game utility.
