# Amphibious Flotation and Suspension Jump Mechanics

We decided to implement continuous liquid buoyancy across water and lava via `minecraft:buoyancy` and an instant-tap `Suspension Jump` with a 1.2-second pneumatic recharge cooldown. Rather than dynamic hydroplaning or horse-style charged jumping, the massive 66-inch tires maintain continuous surface displacement (~0.8-block waterline) allowing swimming player boarding, lava lake navigation with elevated thermal rider shielding, airborne forest canopy demolition, and a downward crush stomp upon landing.
