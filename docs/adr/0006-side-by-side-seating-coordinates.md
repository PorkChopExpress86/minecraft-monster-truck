# Side-by-Side Dual Seat Placement

We decided to position the driver seat at `[-0.45, 1.65, 0.15]` (controlling seat 0) and the passenger seat at `[0.45, 1.65, 0.15]` (seat 1) with `third_person_camera_radius: 7.5` in `minecraft:rideable`. This elevated seating positions rider eye-lines directly level with the transparent windshield opening for an unobstructed cab interior perspective, while providing a high-angle third-person chase camera over the roof and tailgate.
