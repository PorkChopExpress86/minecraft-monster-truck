# Side-by-Side Dual Seat Placement

We decided to position the driver seat at `[-0.55, 1.75, 0.20]` (controlling seat 0) and the passenger seat at `[0.55, 1.75, 0.20]` (seat 1) in `minecraft:rideable`. This side-by-side arrangement mimics standard truck cabin geometry, preserves forward sightlines for both players, and maintains symmetrical third-person camera framing.
