# Side-by-Side Dual Seat Placement

We decided to position the driver seat at `[0.45, 1.15, 0.15]` (controlling seat 0, aligned with the front-left door and `wheel_fl`) and the passenger seat at `[-0.45, 1.15, 0.15]` (seat 1, aligned with `wheel_fr`) with `third_person_camera_radius: 7.5` in `minecraft:rideable`. Setting $Y=1.15$ places the driver's first-person eye height at $Y \approx 40.8$ model units, centered squarely in the transparent windshield opening ($Y=34\text{--}46$) with $>5$ units of vertical headroom below the roof ($Y=46$) and clear sightlines forward over the sloped hood. Re-aligning the lateral $X$ coordinates ensures approaching the left driver door reliably enters Seat 0.
